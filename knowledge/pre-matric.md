# Pre-Matric Scholarship for ST Students

> SAMPLE CONTENT. Replace with the official MoTA guidelines before production use.

## Eligibility
- Student must belong to a Scheduled Tribe.
- Studying in Class IX or X in a recognised school.
- Parents'/guardians' annual income must not exceed the ceiling in the current guidelines (commonly Rs 2.5 lakh).

## Documents
- Caste/Tribe certificate, income certificate, previous marksheet, bank account details (Aadhaar-seeded for DBT), school bonafide certificate.

## Application process
- Register and apply on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP), then the institution and district/state nodal officers verify.

## Benefits
- Includes a monthly/annual allowance and, where applicable, an ad-hoc grant, paid by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
