# Post-Matric Scholarship for ST Students

> SAMPLE CONTENT. Replace with the official MoTA guidelines before production use.

## Eligibility
- Student must belong to a Scheduled Tribe.
- Studying a post-matriculation (Class XI onwards) course in a recognised institution.
- Parents'/guardians' annual income must not exceed the ceiling in the current guidelines (commonly Rs 2.5 lakh).

## Documents
- Tribe certificate, income certificate, previous year marksheet, admission/fee receipt, bank account details (Aadhaar-seeded), institution bonafide certificate.

## Application process
- Apply on NSP. Flow: student -> institute verification -> district/state nodal officer verification -> sanction -> DBT payment.

## Common reasons for deficiency
- Unclear or expired certificates, name mismatch between documents, bank account not Aadhaar-seeded, wrong course/year details.

## Benefits
- Maintenance allowance (depends on course group and hosteller/day-scholar status) plus compulsory non-refundable fees, paid by DBT.
