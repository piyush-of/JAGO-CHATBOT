## What does "District Verification Pending" mean?
Your institute has verified the application and it is waiting for the district nodal officer. You normally do not need to do anything. Contact your institute if it stays pending for a long time.

## What does "Sanctioned" or "DBT Processed" mean?
The scholarship is approved and the amount has been sent to your Aadhaar-seeded bank account. Banks may take a few days to show the credit.

## What should I do if my application has a deficiency?
Open your application on the portal, read the deficiency remark, correct or re-upload the document and resubmit within the deadline.

## My payment has not arrived. What should I check?
Check that your bank account is Aadhaar-seeded and active, that the application is sanctioned, and that your bank details match your documents.

## Never share OTPs, passwords or your full Aadhaar number in chat.
JAGO will never ask for them.
