# Top Class Education Scholarship for ST Students

> SAMPLE CONTENT. Replace with the official MoTA guidelines before production use.

## Eligibility
- ST student admitted to a notified top-class institution for a degree or PG course.
- Family income must be within the ceiling in the current guidelines (commonly Rs 6 lakh).

## Documents
- Tribe certificate, income certificate, admission letter from the notified institution, previous marksheets, bank details.

## Benefits
- Covers tuition/non-refundable fees, a living allowance and funds for books and computer, as per the scheme guidelines.
