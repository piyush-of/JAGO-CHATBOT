# National Overseas Scholarship (NOS) for ST Students

> SAMPLE CONTENT. Replace with the official MoTA guidelines before production use.

## Eligibility
- ST student seeking admission to a Master's or Ph.D programme abroad.
- Family income within the ceiling in the current guidelines (commonly Rs 6 lakh); limited number of awards per year.

## Documents
- Tribe certificate, income certificate, offer letter from the foreign university, academic records, passport, bank details.

## Application process
- Apply through the official portal in the window announced by the Ministry. Selection is merit-based and award numbers are limited.
